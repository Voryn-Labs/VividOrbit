### Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
```
VividOrbit is a sleek, minimalist, and ultra-responsive Live TV channel manager and electronic program guide (EPG) built natively for Android TV and Google TV.

Take back control of your TV experience. Whether you want to renumber your channels, hide channels you never watch, build a custom favorites lineup, or customize your remote buttons—VividOrbit does it all with zero clutter.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

📺 Seamless Live TV Experience
• Instant channel zapping with custom CH+ / CH- key mapping.
• Elegant, unobtrusive Info Banner showing current and upcoming show details.
• Clean, high-performance Channel Guide with category filtering.

🔢 Custom Channel Numbers & Lineups
• Number channels the way YOU want—assign your own channel numbers.
• Smart conflict swap: effortlessly swap numbers between channels.
• 1-to-N Auto-Renumbering: sequentially renumber your entire lineup in one click.
• Hide unwanted channels to keep your guide clean.

📱 Phone Setup via Local Wi-Fi
• Scan a quick QR code on your TV screen to open the web manager on your phone or laptop.
• Reorder, hide, and manage your channels easily from your phone browser.
• Private & 100% Local: Runs directly on your TV box over your local home Wi-Fi. No cloud servers, no account required.

🎮 Full Remote Control Customization
• Map any remote button to Open Guide, Zap Up/Down, Show Info, or Quick Recall.
• Fast recall: switch back to your last watched channel with a single keypress.

🔒 Privacy First & Open Source
• 100% Offline & Private: No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, and no user tracking.
• Open Source: Built using open-source components from the Android Open Source Project (AOSP) and Mochitif Leanback Engine.

⚙️ REQUIREMENTS:
• Android TV / Google TV device running Android 9.0 (Pie) or higher.
• A compatible TV input tuner source or DVB/IPTV provider with TV listings.

Developed with ❤️ by Voryn Labs.
Support & Feedback: appsvorynlabs@gmail.com
```
